# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how BlackLemon Studios ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use our website, web tools and apps (together, the "Services"). We are based in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia and aim to handle personal data in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and, where they apply to you, other data-protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA. By using the Services you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Who we are

BlackLemon Studios is an independent software studio based in Sydney, NSW, Australia. For any privacy question or request, contact our Privacy Officer, **Sri Gorthi**, at **blacklemonstudios.dev@gmail.com**.

## 2. Information we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

- **Information you give us.** When you send feedback or a message, we collect your name, email address, selected product and message. When you post a review, we collect your nickname, rating, title and review text. When you subscribe to our mailing list, we collect your email address.
- **Purchase information.** If you buy a paid app, our payment provider collects and processes your payment details. We receive confirmation of the purchase (such as order reference and the email used) but **do not receive or store your full card details**.
- **Usage and device information.** We may collect limited, mostly aggregated technical information such as approximate download counts, browser type and general interaction data to keep the Services working and improve them.
- **Local storage.** We store small pieces of information in your browser's local storage — for example, a record that you have agreed to our Terms and Privacy Policy, and interface preferences — so you don't have to repeat actions on each visit.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and we ask that you do not submit any in reviews or messages.

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate and maintain the Services;
- respond to your feedback, questions and support requests;
- display published reviews;
- process and deliver purchases of paid apps;
- send you marketing emails **only if you have subscribed**, and let you unsubscribe at any time;
- remember your consent and preferences;
- keep the Services secure, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations; and
- understand and improve how the Services are used.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your **consent** (for marketing and for storing non-essential information); performance of a **contract** (to deliver a paid app you bought); our **legitimate interests** (to run, secure and improve the Services); and **legal obligations** where applicable. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already carried out.

## 5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the Services, and only as needed. These include:

- **Vercel** — website hosting and delivery;
- **Cloudflare** — content delivery, DNS and security;
- **Google Firebase** (Google) — backend services used by some of our apps;
- **Google Fonts** and **Fontshare** — web fonts (your browser may request font files from them);
- **Web3Forms** — receiving and routing the feedback messages you send us;
- **Kit** (formerly ConvertKit) — managing our mailing list if you subscribe;
- **GitHub** (Microsoft) — storing published reviews; and
- **Lemon Squeezy** — processing payments for paid apps as Merchant of Record.

Each provider processes your data under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety and property of BlackLemon Studios, our users or the public.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's **local storage** for essential and preference purposes (such as recording your agreement to our Terms and Privacy Policy, and remembering interface choices). Some third-party services we use may set their own cookies or storage; their use is governed by their own policies. You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings, though doing so may reset preferences and prompt the agreement notice again.

## 7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. In particular, for public reviews we retain only a limited number of the **most recent** reviews on the site; older reviews are deleted automatically. Feedback messages and mailing-list data are kept until they are no longer needed or until you ask us to delete them.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; have it corrected or updated; have it deleted; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; and receive a copy of your data. To exercise any of these, email **blacklemonstudios.dev@gmail.com** and we will respond within a reasonable time and as required by law. If you are in Australia, you may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC); if you are elsewhere, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Services are not directed at anyone under the age of 19, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with applicable law.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, may highlight them in the Services. Please check back periodically.

## 13. Contact

For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact our Privacy Officer, **Sri Gorthi**, at **blacklemonstudios.dev@gmail.com**.
